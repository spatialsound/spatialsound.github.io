Features

Spatialize your next immersive show.

AVINCE turns any club, festival or venue into a 3D sound space, mapping the room, routing your speakers and letting you move sound objects in real time to create an immersive envelop around your audience.

Map any room in minutes

Our system is adaptable to any layouts and calibrates to arbitrary loudspeaker configurations, so you get consistent 3D coverage using the speakers you already have.

Gesture-based mixing in AR

Operators control the mix with intuitive hand gestures in AR, grabbing and moving sound sources directly in 3D while hearing the result instantly as it moves through the room.

Layered spatial sound

Even standard stereo or mono material becomes immersive. AVINCE separates your tracks into placeable elements so existing sets transform into full 3D experiences.

How it works

From mono/stereo rig to 3D venue in three steps.

01

Map and calibrate your venue

We map your room geometry and loudspeaker layout, then automatically tune the system for optimal 3D coverage over your existing sound system.

02

Connect your audio sources

Route decks, mixers, and audio channels into AVINCE. Keep your familiar workflow: AVINCE simply adds a spatial layer on top.

03

Perform and mix in 3D

DJs and engineers control spatial trajectories live with AR gestures while our renderer distributes sound across the room in real time, so the audience hears the set wrap around them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AVINCE?

AVINCE is a spatial audio platform for live events. We map your venue, connect to your existing sound system and let operators control 3D sound in real time using an intuitive AR interface running on Apple Vision Pro.

Do we need to rebuild our sound system?

Not if each of your speakers has an independent audio channel. AVINCE is designed to work with the loudspeakers you already have. We support arbitrary loudspeaker configurations and handle the routing and rendering so you can upgrade to spatial audio without rebuilding the room.

Who is AVINCE for?

We focus on clubs, festivals, concert halls and immersive art spaces that want to differentiate their experience, as well as DJs and live electronic performers looking to push their sets into true 3D.

Does AVINCE introduce latency?

AVINCE is built for real-time performance. The system is engineered to keep latency imperceptible for DJs and live acts, so what you do on the mixer translates instantly into the room.
